# Shelf features

Shelf is a private, local-first home for the pages you want to keep. Save useful links, organize them your way, find them again quickly, and preserve important pages for later—all from a focused desktop app with no account required.

## Your bookmark library, on your computer

- **Local-first ownership** — Your library stays on your computer instead of being placed in a hosted account.

- **No sign-up required** — Install Shelf and start saving bookmarks without creating an account or connecting a cloud service.

- **Portable data** — Choose where Shelf stores its standard SQLite database so it remains easy to inspect with common tools, copy, and back up.

- **Works without a constant connection** — Browse, organize, search, and read saved copies without needing Shelf to connect to an online service.

## Save rich, useful bookmarks

- **Quick bookmark creation** — Add a web page and keep its title, description, address, site details, cover image, author, and publication date when available.

- **One-click page details** — Ask Shelf to retrieve useful information from a page, preview the result, and adjust anything before saving.

- **Refresh page information** — Update a bookmark's page details later without losing its tags, collection, notes, or other organization.

- **Favorites and archive** — Mark important pages as favorites, or move older bookmarks out of the main library without deleting them.

- **Personal notes** — Add your own context, ideas, reminders, or quotes to any bookmark.

## Find anything quickly

- **Search across your whole library** — Find bookmarks by title, description, URL, website, tags, collection, author, site name, or personal notes.

- **Typo-friendly results** — Fuzzy matching helps surface the right bookmark even when your search is not an exact match.

- **Search inside saved pages** — Shelf can find words in the visible text of pages for which you have kept a saved copy.

- **Powerful query options** — Use `#tag` searches, combine terms with `AND`, `OR`, and parentheses, or exclude unwanted matches with syntax such as `rust -wasm`.

- **Saved queries** — Name a useful search once and rerun it later against the latest version of your library.

- **Fast search shortcut** — Press Command/Ctrl+K to jump directly to search.

- **Simple sharing** — Open a bookmark's actions menu to show its QR code, copy its link, or copy its saved details for sharing elsewhere.

- **Short-link cleanup** — Shelf expands redirected links before saving when the destination is reachable, helping prevent duplicate bookmarks hidden behind different short URLs.

## Organize the way you think

- **Tags with autocomplete** — Add multiple tags to a bookmark and reuse existing tags without creating accidental variations.

- **All tags in one place** — Browse every tag from the sidebar and see how many bookmarks use it.

- **Rename tags everywhere** — Find a tag with the searchable picker and rename it across all matching bookmarks in one step.

- **Color-coded collections** — Group bookmarks into named collections and give each collection a recognizable color.

- **Easy collection management** — Create, rename, recolor, or remove collections without deleting their bookmarks.

- **Bulk organization** — Search for a group of bookmarks and move the complete matching set to a collection at once.

- **Flexible filters** — Combine search, tags, and collections with a date-added range, multiple domains from a searchable picker, saved-copy availability, favorites, never-opened bookmarks, notes, missing tags, or archived bookmarks.

## Keep pages for later

- **Saved page copies** — Keep a local copy of an important page while adding a bookmark or at any time afterward.

- **Read saved copies in Shelf** — Open a saved page directly inside the app when the live page is unavailable or you want to revisit the captured version.

- **Refresh or remove copies** — Replace an older copy with a newer capture, or delete the local copy without deleting the bookmark.

- **Export individual pages** — Save a captured page elsewhere whenever you need a separate copy.

- **Clear progress indicators** — See when a copy is being saved, refreshed, or is ready to open.

## Browse your way

- **Grid and list layouts** — Choose visual cards for comfortable browsing or a compact list for faster scanning.

- **Useful sorting** — Sort by newest, oldest, title, website, or most visited.

- **Visit history** — Shelf remembers how often and how recently you opened a bookmark, making frequently used pages easier to surface.

- **Designed for large libraries** — Search and scrolling remain responsive as your collection grows.

## Bring your bookmarks with you

- **Import from Shelf or Raindrop.io** — Move an existing CSV library into Shelf through a guided importer.

- **Review problems before importing** — See file and row issues before your library changes, then cancel or continue with only valid entries.

- **Duplicate-aware imports** — Shelf reports which bookmarks were added, updated, or skipped.

- **Export whenever you want** — Export your full library or only the bookmarks matching the current search and filters.

- **Keep important details** — Shelf's CSV format preserves bookmark metadata, tags, collections, notes, favorites, archive state, and visit information.

## Privacy controls

- **Optional app lock** — Protect access to Shelf with a password when you share your computer or want an extra privacy barrier.

- **Automatic locking** — Choose how long Shelf can remain inactive before it locks itself.

- **Lock immediately** — Secure the app from the toolbar whenever you step away.

The app lock controls access through Shelf; it does not encrypt the library, saved pages, or exported files on disk.

## Made for the desktop

- **macOS, Windows, and Linux** — Shelf is designed as a native desktop experience across the major desktop operating systems.

- **Responsive interface** — The layout adapts to smaller desktop windows while keeping navigation and actions accessible.

- **Native file dialogs and browser opening** — Shelf follows familiar operating-system behavior for choosing files and folders and opening live pages.

- **Accessible, offline-friendly interface** — Keyboard-capable controls, reduced-motion support, and locally bundled interface assets keep Shelf comfortable and dependable.

## Good to know

- Shelf does not currently provide cloud backup, multi-device synchronization, shared libraries, or collaboration.

- Saved copies preserve the captured page, but pages that depend on external online resources may not look or behave exactly like the live site while offline.

- Shelf is currently focused on macOS, Windows, and Linux desktop computers.
